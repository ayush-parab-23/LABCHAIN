How report verification works

A tamper-evident pipeline connecting laboratory issuance with hospital verification.

Step 01

Report issuance

The clinical diagnostic laboratory generates the authenticated medical PDF report for the patient.

Step 02

SHA-256 fingerprint

The system computes a 256-bit cryptographic digest representing the exact byte content of the document.

Step 03

Ledger anchoring

The digest, Report ID, and timestamp are committed immutably onto the Ethereum smart contract.

Step 04

Point-of-care verification

Clinicians scan the report QR code or upload the PDF to perform real-time verification against the ledger.

Security architecture

Architected for healthcare privacy standards and mathematical proof of authenticity.

Zero clinical data on-chain

Patient identifiers and diagnostic values remain strictly off-chain in encrypted storage. Only irreversible cryptographic hashes reside on the blockchain ledger, ensuring patient privacy compliance.

Bit-level tamper detection

Because the SHA-256 algorithm produces an avalanche effect, changing even a single digit or comma inside a diagnostic report completely alters its hash, instantly failing ledger verification.

Immutable version tracking

Reports cannot be overwritten. When a correction is issued, the previous version is marked SUPERSEDED on-chain, creating a transparent, auditable history of medical modifications.
